## Data controller

AWSD, represented by Jean-Vivien Sicot, who can be reached by e-mail through the [Contact](@/contact.en.md) page.

## Data collected

The site has no form, no user account and no advertising. The only data processed is what is contained in e-mails sent to AWSD, used only to reply to them.

## Cookies

No cookies and no browser storage.

## Retention period

E-mails are kept for as long as it takes to handle the request, then archived or deleted.

## Your rights

- Access, rectification and erasure
- Restriction of and objection to processing
- Complaint to the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr), the French data protection authority

These rights are exercised by e-mail.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Scaleway SAS, on servers located in the European Union. The server's technical logs are processed in this context.
